# /build-future-capability

Move from scattered signals to a bounded capability investment decision without allowing upstream `PROPOSAL`, `TARGET`, `ESTIMATE`, client-specific proof, or incomplete business-case evidence to become platform commitment through executive packaging.

## Cross-Skill Lineage Consumer Contract

When opportunity research, client proof, discovery, or a business case supplies claim IDs / a `Reliability Handoff`:

- preserve stable IDs for restated claims;
- preserve state, scope, freshness, estimate method, contradictions, publishability, and restrictions;
- do not promote inherited claims merely because leadership approves a pilot/investigation;
- a decision to invest is a fact about the **decision**, not proof that market/customer/outcome claims became true;
- claims of recurring demand, reuse, right-to-win, and platform readiness derived from multiple inputs get new IDs with parent IDs;
- one client proof point cannot become market recurrence by scope expansion;
- inherited unresolved P0 blockers remain visible at capability gates;
- repeated internal references to the same source do not count as independent demand signals.

## Workflow

### 1. Frame the Decision

Capture the capability hypothesis, target customers, strategic objective, current evidence, constraints, upstream claim IDs/coverage, and what leadership must decide.

### 2. Build the Opportunity Radar

Apply **capability-opportunity-radar**:
- independent demand signals
- problem recurrence and economic consequence
- market/alternative search with contradiction pass
- internal assets and right-to-win
- reuse/productization path

Do not treat one RFP, one client, one case study, one technology trend, or duplicated references to the same underlying claim as sufficient demand proof.

Any cross-account recurrence claim must identify its parent claim IDs and the independence of those evidence sources.

### 3. Decide the Reuse Level

Apply **reusable-accelerator-thesis**:
- bespoke vs method vs accelerator vs productized solution vs platform vs buy/partner
- common core versus client variation
- reuse economics and maintenance burden
- adoption by delivery/sales teams

A `PROPOSAL` or pilot asset remains narrower than reusable IP until reuse evidence exists.

### 4. Build Business Case

Apply **solution-business-case** or ingest a prior business-case handoff:
- decision requested
- alternatives including do nothing and buy/partner
- reachable market/pipeline, not TAM theatre
- conservative/base/upside economics
- sensitivity analysis
- pilot contract and kill criteria

Preserve every inherited estimate/unknown/target and claim ID. Do not reconstruct the same evidence as stronger because it appears in a business case.

### 5. Red-Team

Challenge:
- Is demand source-concentrated?
- Are supposed independent signals actually duplicates of the same account/source?
- Are we productizing consulting custom work that will never standardize?
- Does a vendor/open-source option destroy our right-to-win?
- Is the reusable layer valuable to customers or only convenient internally?
- What if sales demand is 50% lower and implementation effort is 2x higher?
- Which inherited P0 claims would invalidate the capability if they remain unproven?

### 6. Produce Capability Packet

```text
Decision: GO | PILOT | HOLD | KILL | BUY/PARTNER
Problem / ICP:
Independent demand evidence (claim IDs):
Right-to-win (claim IDs/state):
Reuse classification:
Business case range (ESTIMATE preserved):
Pilot:
Inherited P0 hard gates:
Kill criteria:
Owner:
Next leadership decision:
```

Also produce a 1-page executive brief and detailed evidence appendix when enough evidence exists.

### 7. Reliability Handoff

```text
Coverage: COMPLETE FOR DECLARED SCOPE | PARTIAL | BLOCKED

### Reused Claims
| Claim ID | Claim | State | Scope | Capability Use | Restriction |

### Derived Capability Claims
| Claim ID | Parent IDs | Derivation | State | Caveats |

### Independent Signal Map
| Demand Claim | Parent Evidence IDs | Independent? | Reason |

### Unresolved P0
[Claim IDs + blocker + evidence needed]

### Decision Status
[capability investment decision, separate from truth state of underlying claims]

### Prohibited Interpretations
[e.g. PILOT approval != validated demand; client success != reusable IP; roadmap funding != platform proof]
```

## Rule

If evidence is insufficient, the correct output is a validation plan, not a confident business case or roadmap commitment. Executive approval never silently promotes upstream evidence states.
