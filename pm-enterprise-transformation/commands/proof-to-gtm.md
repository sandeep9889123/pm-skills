# /proof-to-gtm

Turn delivered work into a repeatable commercial asset without inventing claims.

## Workflow

### 1. Extract and verify proof
Apply **client-proof-extractor** to reconstruct:
- customer archetype and problem
- delivered intervention
- measured/client-confirmed outcomes
- attribution limits
- confidentiality/publication status

Stop unsupported metrics from entering the GTM layer.

### 2. Test transferability
Ask whether the mechanism applies to another account:
- same job/problem?
- same buyer?
- comparable data/integration prerequisites?
- comparable regulatory/process context?
- outcome valuable enough to fund?

If transferability is weak, classify the story `ACCOUNT-SPECIFIC`.

### 3. Build GTM package
Apply **case-study-to-gtm**:
- ICP + anti-ICP
- trigger event
- pain/consequence/value chain
- verified proof story
- discovery and qualification questions
- objections and evidence
- demo/proof narrative
- asset and distribution plan
- stage metrics

### 4. Check existing-account expansion
When relevant, apply **account-expansion-play** to identify adjacent problems worth discovery without jeopardizing delivery trust.

### 5. Red-team
- Did we confuse acceptance with business success?
- Did we turn a target into an achieved result?
- Does one reference client really represent the ICP?
- Are sensitive details leaking into collateral?
- Could the success be explained by another concurrent change?
- Would sales over-promise beyond the delivered scope?

## Output

Produce:
1. evidence ledger;
2. NDA-safe case-study core;
3. ICP and qualification card;
4. 30-second and 2-minute sales talk tracks;
5. discovery questions;
6. proof/demo plan;
7. objection matrix;
8. GTM asset/distribution plan;
9. conversion/learning metrics;
10. evidence gaps and clearance needs.

### Decision
`SCALE GTM | TEST WITH SELECT PROSPECTS | INTERNAL-SALES-ONLY | ACCOUNT-SPECIFIC | NEEDS EVIDENCE/CLEARANCE`.
