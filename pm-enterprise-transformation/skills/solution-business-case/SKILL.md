# Solution Business Case

## Purpose

Convert an opportunity into an investment decision. A business case must answer: should we invest, how much, why now, what must be true, how will we prove it, and when should we stop?

## Evidence rules

Never invent market size, revenue, utilization, margin, client interest, delivery savings, or conversion uplift. Label every number `MEASURED`, `SOURCE-BACKED`, `ESTIMATE`, or `ASSUMPTION`.

Use ranges for uncertain values. If a load-bearing input is unknown, show the break-even condition instead of fabricating a point estimate.

## Structure

1. **Decision requested**
   - exact funding/capacity/authorization being requested
   - decision deadline and reason

2. **Problem and evidence**
   - recurring customer/delivery problem
   - independent evidence signals
   - current cost of status quo

3. **Strategic fit and right-to-win**
   - adjacency to capabilities/accounts
   - differentiated advantage
   - alternatives including do-nothing, buy, partner, bespoke delivery

4. **Market/revenue logic**
   - beachhead ICP
   - reachable account universe
   - pricing/monetization hypothesis
   - sales motion and sales-cycle implications
   - avoid TAM theatre: separate theoretical market from reachable pipeline

5. **Economics**
   Model base/bull/bear or conservative/base/upside:
   - build/investment cost
   - ongoing maintenance/support
   - pre-sales and implementation cost
   - expected revenue/gross margin or delivery savings
   - payback and break-even
   - sensitivity to the 3 most uncertain drivers

6. **Execution plan**
   - 0→1 pilot
   - reusable proof
   - productization
   - GTM readiness
   - scale conditions

7. **Risks and failure modes**
   For each: probability/confidence, impact, evidence, mitigation, trigger.

8. **Pilot contract**
   Define design-partner/customer criteria, success metrics, false-positive/false-negative risks where applicable, quality gates, timeline, owner, and kill criteria.

## Red-team gate

Before recommendation, answer:
- what is the strongest reason not to invest?
- what assumption is doing the most work?
- what is cheaper than this proposal?
- what happens if sales demand is half the base case?
- what happens if implementation cost is 2×?
- what evidence would change the recommendation?

## Output

### Executive decision memo
`INVEST | PILOT | HOLD | KILL | BUY/PARTNER`

Include amount/capacity requested, expected value range, downside exposure, 3 load-bearing assumptions, cheapest next proof, and revisit trigger.

A polished narrative cannot compensate for an unevidenced economic model.
