# Account Expansion Play

## Purpose

Find the next legitimate customer problem to solve after a successful delivery. Do not convert account familiarity into an assumption that every adjacent solution should be sold.

## Method

1. **Establish delivered trust**
   - what was delivered and adopted?
   - what outcomes are verified?
   - where is satisfaction/usage weak?
   - what commitments remain incomplete?

2. **Map account system**
   - economic buyer
   - champions and users
   - detractors/blockers
   - adjacent teams/business units
   - systems/workflows touching the delivered solution
   - strategic priorities and known transformation programs

3. **Build white-space hypotheses**
   Sources:
   - unresolved problems from original discovery
   - adjacent workflows with the same data/integration foundation
   - repeated manual work
   - adoption bottlenecks
   - compliance/operational risks
   - capabilities already built but underused
   - new business priorities

4. **Score opportunity fit**
   Evaluate problem evidence, strategic relevance, stakeholder access, urgency, budget plausibility, delivery adjacency, reuse leverage, implementation burden, proof available, and risk of damaging trust.

5. **Challenge the expansion thesis**
   - are we solving a real customer problem or trying to sell inventory?
   - does the current solution need stabilization first?
   - is another vendor/team better positioned?
   - is the buyer different?
   - would the expansion increase concentration or delivery risk?

6. **Choose motion**
   `DISCOVERY`, `EXECUTIVE WORKSHOP`, `ASSESSMENT`, `PILOT`, `UPSELL`, `CROSS-SELL`, `NO ACTION`.

## Output

### Account evidence summary
Separate `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, `ASSUMPTION`, `UNKNOWN`.

### Expansion map
| Opportunity | Problem evidence | Stakeholders | Why now | Proof/reuse | Friction | Confidence | Next motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Discovery questions
Questions must test the hypothesis rather than pitch the solution.

### Guardrails
Never infer confidential strategy from weak clues; never claim budget exists without evidence; never recommend expansion that jeopardizes delivery trust.

### Decision
`PURSUE | DISCOVER | HOLD | DO NOT PURSUE` with trigger for revisit.
