# Evidence-Backed Account Expansion Play

## Purpose

Find the next legitimate customer problem to solve after delivery. Account familiarity, executive access, positive sentiment, or one successful PoC are not expansion proof.

## P0 Reliability Contract

1. Separate `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, `ASSUMPTION`, `ESTIMATE`, `UNKNOWN`, `STALE`, and `TARGET`.
2. Do not invent budget, urgency, stakeholder authority, strategic priority, buying intent, adoption, realized outcome, reuse leverage, or implementation readiness.
3. Positive relationship sentiment and meeting enthusiasm are not commercial commitment.
4. Expansion is blocked when the current delivery has unresolved trust-critical, adoption, quality, security, or contractual issues that could be worsened by selling more scope.
5. A successful pilot does not prove production adoption, renewal, repeatability, or referenceability.
6. Verify who the user, champion, economic buyer, approver, procurement/security stakeholders, and blockers are. Do not assume the original buyer owns the adjacent problem.
7. Reuse is an economic/technical hypothesis until common-core evidence and customization burden are known.
8. Compare `PURSUE`, `DISCOVER`, `PARTNER/REFER`, and `NO ACTION`; do not optimize for selling inventory.
9. Account concentration and delivery-capacity risk must be explicit when expansion increases exposure to one client.
10. Tool/search/internal-data failure means `COVERAGE INCOMPLETE / UNKNOWN`.

## Step 1: Establish Current-Delivery Truth

Capture:
- contracted vs delivered scope
- adoption/usage evidence
- verified outcomes and attribution limits
- open defects / commitments / escalations
- support and implementation burden
- stakeholder satisfaction evidence
- renewal / commercial status if known

Classify current account health:
`STABLE | STABILIZE FIRST | AT RISK | UNKNOWN`

`STABILIZE FIRST` or `AT RISK` blocks opportunistic upsell unless the expansion directly resolves the trust problem and the customer explicitly supports that path.

## Step 2: Map the Account System

Separate:
- users/operators
- champions
- economic buyer
- approvers
- procurement/security/legal/IT
- detractors/blockers
- adjacent teams/business units
- systems and workflows touching the delivered capability

Unknown roles stay `UNKNOWN`.

## Step 3: Build White-Space Hypotheses

Potential sources:
- unresolved problems from original discovery
- adjacent workflow evidence
- repeated manual work
- adoption bottlenecks
- compliance/operational risks
- explicit stakeholder asks
- new verified business priorities
- capabilities already delivered but underused

Every white-space item begins as `HYPOTHESIS` unless direct evidence supports it.

## Step 4: Opportunity Evidence

For each opportunity capture:

| Opportunity | Problem evidence | Buyer/owner | Why now | Current alternative | Budget/WTP evidence | Delivery fit | Reuse proof | Risk | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Do not use a numerical score unless inputs and weighting are defined. `SUPPORTED | PARTIAL | UNKNOWN | CONTRADICTED` is usually safer.

## Step 5: Contradiction / Trust Pass

Ask:
- Are we solving a real customer problem or trying to sell available capability?
- Is the current solution stable enough to deserve expansion?
- Does only one friendly stakeholder support the idea?
- Is the adjacent buyer different?
- Is budget assumed from account size?
- Is implementation highly custom or capacity-heavy?
- Would another vendor/team be objectively better positioned?
- Could expansion increase concentration, margin, support, or delivery risk?
- Does the customer have a cheaper internal/configuration/manual alternative?

## Step 6: Choose the Next Motion

Valid motions:
`DISCOVERY | EXECUTIVE WORKSHOP | ASSESSMENT | PILOT | COMMERCIAL PROPOSAL | PARTNER/REFER | HOLD | NO ACTION`

Use `COMMERCIAL PROPOSAL` only when problem, buyer, readiness, and commercial pathway are sufficiently supported. Otherwise prefer discovery.

## Step 7: Expansion Gate

Decision:
`PURSUE | DISCOVER | STABILIZE FIRST | HOLD | DO NOT PURSUE`

State:
- strongest evidence for expansion
- strongest rejection case
- unresolved P0 blockers
- what evidence would change the decision
