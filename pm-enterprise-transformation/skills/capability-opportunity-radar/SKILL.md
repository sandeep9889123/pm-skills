# Capability Opportunity Radar

## Purpose

Decide which recurring customer or delivery problems deserve investment as a reusable capability rather than another one-off project.

Do not start from technology enthusiasm. Start from repeated pain, reachable demand, evidence of delivery advantage, and a plausible path to reuse.

## Reliability contract

Separate every material input into `FACT`, `INFERENCE`, `ASSUMPTION`, `ESTIMATE`, `UNKNOWN`, or `STALE`.

A single client request is not market demand. A market report is not right-to-win. A technically feasible demo is not a business case. Repeated internal enthusiasm is not external willingness to pay.

Before recommending investment, run a contradiction pass: what evidence suggests this should remain bespoke, be bought/partnered, or not be pursued?

## Method

1. **Define the opportunity arena**
   - recurring customer problem/job
   - buyer and user
   - industries/geographies
   - current alternatives and manual workarounds
   - why the opportunity matters now

2. **Build signal inventory**
   Capture evidence from:
   - client requests/RFPs/RFIs
   - delivery retrospectives and recurring custom work
   - win/loss and sales-call patterns
   - market/competitor signals
   - technology/regulatory shifts
   - internal assets, skills, datasets, accelerators, partnerships

3. **De-duplicate signals**
   Multiple mentions from one client or one sales pursuit do not automatically equal independent demand. Identify source concentration.

4. **Assess each opportunity** across:
   - problem recurrence
   - pain/economic consequence
   - willingness-to-pay evidence
   - reachable market
   - strategic adjacency
   - reuse potential across accounts
   - differentiation/right-to-win
   - data/integration readiness
   - delivery feasibility
   - time-to-proof
   - productization effort
   - sales enablement burden
   - operational/support burden
   - downside if wrong

5. **Score with confidence, not fake precision**
   Use High/Medium/Low or 1–5 only when evidence supports the distinction. Every score needs evidence and confidence. Do not manufacture decimal rankings.

6. **Classify the opportunity**
   - `BESPOKE`: keep project-specific
   - `PATTERN`: reusable method/templates but not software/IP
   - `ACCELERATOR`: reusable assets meaningfully reduce time/risk
   - `PRODUCTIZED SOLUTION`: repeatable offer with bounded customization
   - `PLATFORM CAPABILITY`: shared foundation supports multiple solutions/products
   - `PARTNER/BUY`: market need exists but internal build is not the best path
   - `HOLD`: evidence insufficient

7. **Choose the cheapest next proof**
   Examples: 5-account evidence review, paid discovery, prototype with one design partner, synthetic eval, reuse audit across past projects, pricing conversation, build-vs-buy spike.
