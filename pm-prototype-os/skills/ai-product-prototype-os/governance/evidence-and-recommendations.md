# Evidence / recommendation discipline
Use repository-compatible states FACT, INFERENCE, ASSUMPTION, ESTIMATE, UNKNOWN, STALE, TARGET and PROPOSAL where consequential. Stable claim IDs survive handoffs; no silent promotion or widened scope; carry contradictory evidence and unresolved P0 blockers. Trace user-approved product decisions separately from observed facts. Challenge weak assumptions with disconfirming paths and a reasoned preferred option, not unilateral action. Mark unexecuted tests NOT_RUN and uninspected surfaces COVERAGE INCOMPLETE. Technical feasibility never automatically proves demand, willingness to pay or security.
