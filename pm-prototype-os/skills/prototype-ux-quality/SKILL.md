# Prototype interaction and AI UX quality

For a new task ask 5–10 material questions and await phase approval; continue previously approved scope without another intake. Preserve the canonical contract when available, zero incremental spending, evidence states and the owner's decisions.

Map the approved critical task from entry to verified outcome. Inspect navigation and real controls, loading/empty/error/success/permission states, keyboard/semantic accessibility, viewport behavior and recovery. Separate visual polish, functional demo, simulated backend, real integration and actually tested behavior. Mark unavailable checks NOT_RUN.

For AI interfaces establish supported tasks, limitations, source/as-of context, appropriate uncertainty/abstention, editable outputs, dismissal/correction, escalation and reversible actions. A confident tone is not evidence. Do not expose private retrieved content or present unverified actions as complete.

Plan realistic user tasks with ordinary success, wrong evidence, unsupported questions, a model error and recovery/handoff. Specify participant selection and data restrictions. Observe actual completion, error detection, verification/review effort, correction, abandonment and perceived versus actual capability. Separate observed behavior, participant statements and interpretation; keep negative cases and sample limits. Compare with existing search/manual flow when it answers the research question. Prototype preference is not adoption or willingness-to-pay evidence.

Deliver state/interaction findings, requirement and trace locators, user-study script/observations, accessibility evidence, severity rationale, unknowns and proposed next validation. Recommendations are options for the owner, not implementation authority. Where available, use the complete plugin's prototype-evaluation AI UX research worksheet; all minimum checks above remain usable independently.

Method lead: [Microsoft Research human-AI interaction](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/overview/).
