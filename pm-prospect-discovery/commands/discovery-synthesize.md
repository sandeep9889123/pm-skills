# Discovery Synthesize

Synthesize:

$ARGUMENTS

Use discovery-synthesis, assumption-register, discovery-red-team, and proposal-readiness.

## Required behavior

- Restate the original hypothesis before presenting the result.
- Do not invent answers for unanswered questions.
- Preserve contradictions and participant authority limits.
- Update assumptions as CONFIRMED / DENIED / PARTIAL / UNKNOWN.
- Separate validated Phase 1 scope from future ideas.
- Identify prospect-owned and delivery-owned dependencies.
- List unresolved P0 items and the decision each blocks.
- State the strongest supported reason not to proceed.
- Return separate readiness for solutioning, architecture, estimation, business case, and proposal.
- Recommend one of: PROCEED TO SOLUTIONING, PROCEED WITH CONDITIONS, SECOND DISCOVERY REQUIRED, REFRAME USE CASE, STOP / NO-GO.
