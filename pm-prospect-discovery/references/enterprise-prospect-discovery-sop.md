# Enterprise Prospect Discovery SOP

## Purpose

Standardize high-level pre-RFP discovery so every prospect is evaluated with the same decision discipline while questions remain specific to the account, industry, and proposed use case.

## Trigger

Use this SOP when:

- a prospect has expressed interest but no formal RFP exists yet
- Sales has proposed a solution/use case that needs validation
- the team needs information before solutioning, estimation, business case, or proposal
- discovery has previously been recreated manually for similar opportunities

Do not use it as a replacement for a formal requirements phase after commitment.

## Roles

### Commercial / Sales

Provides account context, relationship history, buying signal, known stakeholders, and commercial constraints. Sales does not unilaterally convert prospect interest into product requirements.

### Product / Solution Discovery Owner

Owns decision framing, hypotheses, discovery quality, question prioritization, assumption register, and synthesis.

### Technical / Architecture Partner

Joins when material system, data, security, integration, or feasibility decisions are in scope.

### Delivery

Joins when ownership, team model, delivery constraints, effort envelope, or dependencies need validation.

### Prospect

Provides operational truth, constraints, decision context, and evidence. Different attendees may know different parts of the system. Unknown is valid.

## Standard process

### Stage 0: Intake

Capture:

- account
- industry
- known problem/signal
- proposed use case, if any
- source material
- known stakeholders
- target meeting duration
- desired next decision

### Stage 1: Research

Build only decision-relevant prospect and industry context. Tag evidence states.

### Stage 2: Hypothesis formation

Produce:

- problem hypothesis
- alternative root causes
- 1-3 candidate use-case wedges
- recommended discovery focus
- evidence that could falsify the preferred path

### Stage 3: Pre-session red team

Challenge problem, value, user/buyer fit, existing alternatives, data, integration, delivery, and buyability.

### Stage 4: Journey and assumptions

Build 5-8 stages plus prioritized P0/P1/P2 assumptions.

### Stage 5: Question design

Create MUST ASK and conditional Level 2 questions. Every mandatory question must unlock a decision.

### Stage 6: Session

Facilitator rules:

- state that the proposed path is a working hypothesis
- ask for current behavior and concrete examples
- capture contradictions
- distinguish known, unknown, and owned-by-someone-else
- do not argue the prospect into confirming an assumption
- capture explicit scope and out-of-scope choices
- capture who owns each dependency

### Stage 7: Synthesis

Within the agreed internal cadence, produce evidence changes, decisions, unresolved P0 items, and next actions.

### Stage 8: Readiness gate

Do not progress automatically. Gate solutioning, architecture, estimation, business case, and proposal independently.

## Resource protection

Discovery should reduce downstream rework. If P0 information is missing, the correct outcome is often a targeted follow-up, not premature engineering involvement.

## Definition of done

A discovery cycle is done when:

- the decision it was designed to enable can be made, or
- the team explicitly concludes that more discovery is required, or
- the use case is reframed/stopped

Completing all questionnaire fields is not the definition of done.
