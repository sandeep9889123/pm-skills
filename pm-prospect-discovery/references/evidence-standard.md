# Prospect Discovery Evidence Standard

## Evidence states

### FACT

Directly supported by authoritative prospect material, a clearly attributable prospect statement, or verified public primary evidence.

A prospect statement can be a fact about what that participant said. It is not automatically proof that the statement is operationally complete or organization-wide.

### INFERENCE

A reasoned conclusion from facts.

### ASSUMPTION

An unverified belief required for the proposed problem, scope, value, architecture, or delivery path.

### ESTIMATE

A quantitative approximation. Include formula, inputs, units, and source/assumption state.

### UNKNOWN

Material information not yet known.

### STALE

Previously known information that may no longer reflect current reality.

## Decision-critical evidence

Treat evidence as P0 when it can materially change:

- whether the use case is worth solving
- user/buyer fit
- Phase 1 scope
- architecture
- data/integration design
- security/compliance
- delivery effort
- economics
- commercial commitment

## Rules

- Never invent missing answers.
- Never convert user enthusiasm into willingness to pay.
- Never convert an internal target into prospect value proof.
- Never convert a vendor capability claim into confirmed prospect feasibility.
- Never convert a sales statement into operational fact without attribution.
- Contradictory evidence remains visible until resolved.
- Tool failure means coverage incomplete, not absence.

## Evidence quality over completeness

An explicit `UNKNOWN` is safer than a plausible but unsupported answer.
