# Enterprise Discovery Question Taxonomy

Use this taxonomy as coverage guidance, not as a mandatory questionnaire.

## 1. Business trigger

Why now, what changed, consequence of inaction, strategic priority.

## 2. User / buyer / operator

Who performs the work, who experiences pain, who approves, who pays, who can veto.

## 3. Current workflow

Specific end-to-end current behavior, handoffs, tools, workarounds.

## 4. Pain and failure modes

Where work breaks, exceptions, delays, errors, rework, escalation, risk.

## 5. Volume / scale

Frequency, transactions, users, locations, peaks, seasonality, exception rate.

## 6. Economics

Time, people effort, revenue leakage, cost, penalties, opportunity cost, current spend.

## 7. Systems / architecture

Systems of record, applications, ownership, hosting boundaries, identity.

## 8. Data

Sources, formats, quality, freshness, history, labels/ground truth, access rights.

## 9. Integrations

APIs, files, database views, events, middleware, vendor constraints, write-back.

## 10. Business rules

Calculations, approvals, thresholds, exceptions, judgment, local variants.

## 11. Security / privacy / compliance

Data sensitivity, access, audit, residency, approval, regulatory controls.

## 12. Ownership / operating model

Who owns data, process, system, approvals, support, change management.

## 13. Buying / decision process

Sponsor, evaluator, approver, procurement, budget owner, timeline, competing priorities.

## 14. Success

Business outcome, user outcome, technical acceptance, operational SLA, guardrails.

## 15. Constraints

Deadline, budget, team, vendor, legal, procurement, architecture, data, rollout.

## 16. Future state / reuse

Adjacent journeys, expansion path, scalability, multi-business applicability.

## Question selection test

Keep a question in MUST ASK only if its answer can change a material decision.

Otherwise move it to Level 2 or remove it.
