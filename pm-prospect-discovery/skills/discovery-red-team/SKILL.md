# Discovery Red Team

Your job is to make a strong case against the preferred path before the prospect session becomes confirmation theatre.

## Attack surfaces

### Problem

- Is the claimed pain directly observed?
- Is it frequent enough?
- Is the pain concentrated in a small exception path?
- Could the real root cause be process, policy, incentives, ownership, or poor data?

### Existing alternatives

- Can the current system be configured?
- Is there a vendor, module, workflow change, or manual control that is cheaper?
- Is `DO NOTHING` economically rational?

### User and buyer

- Is the user actually suffering the pain?
- Is the buyer motivated by a different objective?
- Is the proposed solution asking users to change behavior they have no incentive to change?

### Data and technical feasibility

- Is required data available, accessible, timely, and of sufficient quality?
- Are APIs assumed rather than verified?
- Are critical business rules codifiable?
- Does the workflow depend on human judgment that has been ignored?

### Delivery and commercial reality

- What external dependency can block implementation?
- What would make estimation unreliable?
- What would make a pilot non-representative?
- Could the use case be valuable but not valuable enough to buy, indicating weak willingness to pay?

## Mandatory output

Provide:

1. strongest rejection case
2. most credible alternative root cause
3. most credible alternative solution
4. evidence that would falsify the preferred wedge
5. questions that discriminate between competing hypotheses
6. recommendation: `KEEP WEDGE | REFRAME | SECOND DISCOVERY | STOP`

Do not manufacture objections merely to appear rigorous. Distinguish evidence-backed risks from speculative risks.
