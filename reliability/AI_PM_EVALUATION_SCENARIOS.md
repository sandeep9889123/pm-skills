# AI PM evaluation failure families

These protect procedure contracts; they are not completed runtime tests.

## PO11_LABEL_PROVENANCE

Synthetic or unknown labels are presented as genuine expert judgments.

## PO12_TRACE_DENOMINATOR_BIAS

Complaint-enriched or duplicated traces are reported as population failure prevalence.

## PO13_JUDGE_TEST_LEAKAGE

Development or reused test cases are claimed as untouched judge assessment.

## PO14_JUDGE_CLASS_IMBALANCE

High agreement hides an always-PASS judge that misses failures.

## PO15_RAG_RECALL_UNKNOWN

Recall is invented without a known relevant-document inventory.

## PO16_COST_SUCCESS_DENOMINATOR

Failed attempts, retries or human review vanish from successful-task cost.

## PO17_TELEMETRY_AND_DRIFT

Configured logging is treated as verified telemetry or quality drift cause.

## PO18_VARIANT_CONFOUND

Changed corpus/tool conditions are attributed solely to a model or prompt.

## PO19_TASK_COMPLETION_STATE

An agent completion message overrides unchanged or unauthorized final state.

## PO20_ROUTER_PERMISSION_FALLBACK

A cache or fallback crosses tenant, source-version or action boundaries.
