# /business-model -- Evidence-Backed Business Model Exploration

Use Lean Canvas, Business Model Canvas, Startup Canvas, or Value Proposition as **analytical lenses**, not as permission to fill missing facts with plausible text.

## Step 1: Resolve Mode and Evidence Status

Capture:
- decision this analysis supports
- product/business stage
- customer/buyer/payer
- geography/market scope
- current operating model, if any
- evidence available

Choose:
- `EVIDENCE MODE`: operating/customer/economic evidence exists
- `HYPOTHESIS MODE`: material blocks are not yet verified

In hypothesis mode, label unsupported canvas content `ASSUMPTION`, `PROPOSAL`, or `UNKNOWN`.

## Step 2: Apply the Selected Framework

### Lean
Use **lean-canvas** for early hypotheses. Do not invent market facts, unfair advantage, channels, or economics merely to complete every box.

### Full BMC
Use hardened **business-model**. Preserve evidence states, reconstruct economics where data exists, and compare at least one credible alternative/current-state model.

### Startup
Use **startup-canvas**, but treat strategy, growth, moat, and economics as hypotheses unless supported.

### Value Proposition
Use **value-proposition**. Separate observed JTBD/current alternatives from proposed positioning.

### All
Run multiple frameworks only when comparison adds decision value. **Agreement between frameworks is not independent corroborating evidence** because the same inputs/model can produce correlated outputs. Treat agreement as analytical consistency, not proof.

## Step 3: Economic and Coherence Gate

Where material:
- separate user/buyer/payer
- show revenue/cost formulas and assumptions
- do not invent CAC, LTV, margin, WTP, conversion, retention, or market size
- test channel/ACV/implementation fit
- surface service/support burden
- compare build/buy/partner/current-state where relevant

## Step 4: Contradiction Pass

Ask:
- Which canvas block has the weakest evidence?
- What alternative business model could capture value with less risk?
- Does buyer behavior contradict the proposed revenue/channel model?
- Does delivery cost undermine the revenue model?
- Is “moat” merely a feature/capability assertion?
